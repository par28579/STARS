# Open Questions

Design decisions still being worked out.

## Powers

**Decided:** Powers is its own attribute, and the power system is the skills (disciplines) under it. Powers can be switched off for mundane settings.

Still open:

- **Cost and limits.** What stops a 70% discipline from outclassing a 70% mundane skill? Options: a points pool (mana, strain), a fatigue or condition cost per use, backlash on failures and fumbles, or a mix.
- **Effect scale.** How do worlds define what a discipline can do, and how strong a critical vs. a normal success is?

## Willpower

Resolve moves out of Powers. Proposed home: **Charisma** (force of personality). Alternative: Intellect (mental discipline).

## Endurance

Nothing explicitly covers stamina or physical endurance yet. Fold it into Strength, or handle it elsewhere?

## Damage and health

- Hit points, conditions (Shaken, Wounded, Exhausted), or both?
- How is health derived from attributes?

## Character creation

- How many points do players spend on attributes and skills?
- Any caps on starting skill values?

## Genre dials

Settings a world builder can turn without changing the core rules. Candidates:

- Lethality
- Powers on/off
- Rarity and potency of powers
- Heroic resources (Luck, Grit, and so on)
