# Advancement

*Draft.* Characters improve at **milestones**: points in the story where they've accomplished something that matters. There's no experience point tracking.

## When milestones happen

The GM awards a milestone to the whole group when the story reaches one, such as:

- Completing a major objective (the heist pulled off, the cult exposed, the ship repaired)
- Surviving a turning point (a defeat, a betrayal, a narrow escape)
- Finishing a chapter or story arc

As a guide, a milestone every **2–3 sessions** keeps growth steady without racing. Every **fourth milestone** is a **major milestone**.

## Advancement points

Each milestone gives every character **advancement points**, based on the world's power level:

| Power level | Points per milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Gritty | 10 |
| Standard | 15 |
| Heroic | 20 |

### Spending on skills

Raise a skill's training, paying more as the skill gets higher:

| Skill value being reached | Cost per +1 |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 50 | 1 point |
| 51–75 | 2 points |
| 76 and up | 3 points |

- A skill can rise by at most **+5 per milestone**.
- A character can start training a skill they've never trained, including a new power discipline, as long as it fits what they've been doing in the story.
- Unspent points carry over to the next milestone.

> **Example:** Rafael's Drive is 60. Raising it to 65 costs 5 × 2 = 10 points, leaving him 5 for something else, like Notice from 45 to 50 (5 × 1 = 5).

### Major milestones: attributes

At each major milestone, a character also gains **+5 to one attribute**, up to **90**. Reaching 91–100 takes a world that allows legendary or superhuman characters.

Raising an attribute also raises every skill under it, since skills are half the attribute plus training. Recalculate derived values (HP, movement, power slots) afterward.

## Pacing

Under these rules, in a Standard world, a focused fighter starting with a weapon skill of 60 reaches:

| Skill | Bonus | Around milestone | Around session |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 75 | +1 attack | 3 | 6–9 |
| 100 | +2 attacks | 8 | 16–24 |

Skill 100 takes nearly all of a character's points for several milestones, so it's for true masters, not everyone.
