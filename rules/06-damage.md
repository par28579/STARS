# Damage

*Draft.* When an attack succeeds, the attacker rolls the weapon's **damage dice** and subtracts the total from the target's HP.

## Weapon classes

STARS sorts weapons into classes rather than listing every weapon. Each world assigns its own weapons to a class.

| Class | Damage | Fantasy | Modern | Sci-fi |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Unarmed** | d3 | Fists, kicks | Fists, kicks | Fists, kicks |
| **Light** | d4 | Dagger, club, sling | Knife, baton | Stun baton, vibro-knife |
| **Medium** | d6 | Short sword, mace, shortbow | Pistol, machete | Holdout blaster |
| **Heavy** | d8 | Longsword, battleaxe, longbow | Rifle, submachine gun | Blaster rifle |
| **Very heavy** | d10 | Greatsword, crossbow | Shotgun (close), sniper rifle | Plasma rifle |
| **Massive** | 2d8 | Siege crossbow, giant's club | Heavy machine gun | Mounted cannon |

For reference, a typical character in a Standard world has about 14 HP, so a sword fight lasts a few solid hits.

## Strength bonus

Melee and thrown weapons add a bonus for high Strength:

| Strength | Damage bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Under 70 | +0 |
| 70–89 | +1 |
| 90–100 | +2 |

## Criticals and fumbles

- **Critical:** the attack deals **maximum damage** (no roll), and causes an injury (see [`05-health-and-injuries.md`](05-health-and-injuries.md#injuries)).
- **Fumble:** the attack misses and something goes wrong: the gun jams, the blade sticks, the attacker stumbles.

## Power damage

Damaging powers use their effect tier (see [`04-power-slots.md`](04-power-slots.md#effect-tiers)) instead of a weapon class:

| Tier | Damage | Targets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minor** | d3 | One target |
| **Moderate** | d8 | One target |
| **Major** | 2d6 | Everyone in a small area |
| **Extreme** | 4d8 | Everyone in a large area |

Power criticals and fumbles follow the same rules as weapons, plus slot refunds and backlash.

> **Example:** Mavessa spends 1 slot on a Moderate firebolt and succeeds on her Pyrokinesis test. She rolls d8 for 6 damage. The guard has 14 HP, so the hit doesn't exceed half his max HP (7), and no injury results.
