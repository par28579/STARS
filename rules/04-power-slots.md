# Power Slots

Powers are limited by **slots**: a single shared pool of uses that refreshes with rest. Every discipline a character knows draws from the same pool.

Each world names slots to suit its flavor: spell slots, psi charges, blessings, battery cells, ki.

## How many slots

A character has **one slot per 10 points of Powers** (rounded down).

| Powers | Slots |
| --- | --- |
| 1–9 | 0 |
| 10–19 | 1 |
| 50–59 | 5 |
| 90–99 | 9 |
| 100 | 10 |

A character with 0 slots can still use Minor effects.

## Effect tiers

Every use of a discipline is one of four tiers. Bigger effects cost more slots.

| Tier | Cost | Scale | Fantasy example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Minor** | Free | A trick or small convenience | Light a candle with a fingertip |
| **Moderate** | 1 slot | Useful in a scene; affects one target | Hurl a firebolt at a guard |
| **Major** | 2 slots | Changes a scene; affects a group or area | Fill a hallway with flame |
| **Extreme** | 3 slots | Changes the story | Call down a firestorm on a fortress |

The GM decides the tier of an effect before the roll, so the player knows the cost.

## Spending slots

1. The player declares the effect, and the GM sets its tier.
2. The player spends the slots. **Slots are spent on the attempt**, not only on success.
3. The player makes the discipline test as normal.

| Result | Slots |
| --- | --- |
| **Critical** | The effect works with a bonus, and the slots are **refunded** |
| **Success** | The effect works |
| **Failure** | The effect fails; slots are lost |
| **Fumble** | The effect fails; slots are lost, and there's a **backlash** |

A character can't attempt an effect that costs more slots than they have left.

### Backlash

A backlash is a complication caused by the power itself: the fire spreads the wrong way, the telepath hears too much, the suit overheats. The GM picks one that fits the discipline and the scene. (A more formal backlash table may come later.)

## Recovering slots

All spent slots refresh after a **full rest**. The world decides what a full rest is (see genre dials):

- **Heroic:** a short break
- **Standard:** a full night's sleep
- **Gritty:** a week of downtime
