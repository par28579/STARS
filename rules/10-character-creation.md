# Character Creation

*Draft.* Building a character takes seven steps.

1. Concept
2. Attributes
3. Skills
4. Powers (if the world has them)
5. Derived values
6. Equipment
7. Final details

The world's **power level** (Gritty, Standard or Heroic) sets how many points players get.

## 1. Concept

Start with a sentence or two: who the character is, what they do, and why they're in the story. "A getaway driver who owes the wrong people money." "A disgraced court wizard looking for the book that ruined her." The concept guides every choice after it.

## 2. Attributes

### Point-buy (default)

Every attribute starts at **20**. Then spend points to raise them, one point per point of attribute.

| Power level | Points per attribute | Total with 5 attributes | Total with 4 (Powers off) | Average result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gritty | 15 | 75 | 60 | 35 |
| Standard | 25 | 125 | 100 | 45 |
| Heroic | 35 | 175 | 140 | 55 |

- No attribute can start above **70**.
- You can lower an attribute below 20, down to 10, and spend the points elsewhere.

Since the average person scores 30–35, a Standard character is above average overall, with room to be exceptional at one or two things.

### Rolled (optional)

For each attribute, roll **2d6 × 5 + 10** (range 20–70, average 45), in order. Gritty worlds use 2d6 × 5 (range 10–60). Heroic worlds use 2d6 × 5 + 20 (range 30–80, capped at 70).

## 3. Skills

Every skill starts at **half its attribute** (see [`03-skills.md`](03-skills.md)). Then spend **skill points** on training.

| Power level | Skill points |
| --- | --- |
| Gritty | 150 |
| Standard | 200 |
| Heroic | 250 |

- At most **+40 training** on any one skill.
- No skill can start above **70**. Extra attacks at skill 75 come only through advancement.
- Every character has the core skills (Brawl, Melee, Dodge, Medicine, Resolve), even if untrained.

## 4. Powers

If the world has Powers switched on, power disciplines are skills under Powers. Buy them with the same skill points, under the same limits. A character with Powers but no disciplines has slots and nothing to spend them on, so most power users take at least one.

## 5. Derived values

Work these out from the attributes:

| Value | Formula | See |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HP** | Strength ÷ 5 + the world's HP bonus | [`05-health-and-injuries.md`](05-health-and-injuries.md#hit-points) |
| **Movement** | Average of Strength and Dexterity, in feet | [`09-combat.md`](09-combat.md#movement) |
| **Power slots** | Powers ÷ 10 | [`04-power-slots.md`](04-power-slots.md#how-many-slots) |
| **Strength damage bonus** | +1 at 70, +2 at 90 | [`06-damage.md`](06-damage.md#strength-bonus) |
| **Healing per rest** | Strength ÷ 10 | [`08-healing.md`](08-healing.md#natural-healing) |

## 6. Equipment

Each world sets its starting gear. As a default, a character starts with:

- Clothing and everyday items that fit their concept
- One or two weapons and one suit of armor that fit their concept, approved by the GM
- **3 spare magazines** (or quivers, cells, and so on) for each ranged weapon
- A small amount of money or trade goods, set by the world

## 7. Final details

Name, appearance, personality, and a hook or two for the GM: a goal, a secret, a rival, a debt.

## Example: Rafael Pontes

**Concept:** A rigger in a São Paulo heist crew. Good with cars, locks and staying out of sight.

**World:** Modern, Standard power level, Powers off. So 4 attributes, 100 points, and 200 skill points.

**Attributes** (each starts at 20):

| Attribute | Points | Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | +25 | 45 |
| Dexterity | +40 | 60 |
| Intellect | +30 | 50 |
| Charisma | +5 | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** | |

**Skills** (base is half the attribute):

| Skill | Base | Training | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Athletics (STR) | 22 | +20 | 42 |
| Melee (STR) | 22 | +15 | 37 |
| Brawl (STR) | 22 | — | 22 |
| Drive (DEX) | 30 | +40 | 70 |
| Stealth (DEX) | 30 | +35 | 65 |
| Dodge (DEX) | 30 | +30 | 60 |
| Ranged (DEX) | 30 | +20 | 50 |
| Notice (INT) | 25 | +20 | 45 |
| Medicine (INT) | 25 | +10 | 35 |
| Resolve (CHA) | 12 | +10 | 22 |
| **Total training** | | **200** | |

**Derived values:** 14 HP (9 + 5), 55 ft movement, no Strength damage bonus, 4 HP healed per rest.

**Equipment:** crowbar (Light, d4), pistol (Medium damage, Short range, capacity 10) with 3 spare magazines, leather jacket (AV 1), lockpicks, a beat-up car he keeps running out of spite.
