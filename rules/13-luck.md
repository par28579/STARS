# Luck

*Draft.* In a percentile system, even skilled characters fail often. **Luck points** give players a little control over the dice when it matters most.

Each world can rename Luck to fit its tone: Grit, Fate, Edge, Fortune, Nerve.

## Luck per session

Every player character starts each session with a number of Luck points set by the world's **Luck dial** (see [`12-genre-dials.md`](12-genre-dials.md#luck)):

| Setting | Luck per session |
| --- | --- |
| Off | 0 |
| Gritty | 1 |
| Standard | 3 |
| Heroic | 5 |

Unspent Luck doesn't carry over. Use it or lose it.

## Spending Luck

Spend Luck on your own rolls, after you see the result. Only **one** Luck option can be used on any single roll.

| Option | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flip** | 1 | Swap the tens and ones digits of a d100 roll. A roll of 82 becomes 28 |
| **Reroll** | 1 | Reroll any test or damage roll and take the new result, even if it's worse |
| **Shrug it off** | 1 | Cancel an injury from a hit. You still take the HP damage |
| **Cheat death** | 2 | At 0 HP, you're stable and unconscious instead of dying, and no further damage kills you this scene |

### Flip or reroll?

A flip is a sure thing you can check before deciding: you know exactly what the flipped number will be. A reroll is a gamble. It can fix any roll, including damage, but the new result might be worse.

> **Example:** Rafael tests Stealth 60 and rolls 82, a failure. Flipping makes it 28, a success, so he spends 1 Luck and slips past the guard. If he had rolled 71, flipping would give 17, also a success. If he had rolled 66, flipping wouldn't help, so a reroll is his only option.

### Notes

- **Doubles don't flip.** A roll of 33 or 77 stays the same, so only a reroll can change it.
- **Hit locations** use the final roll after any flip (see [`05-health-and-injuries.md`](05-health-and-injuries.md#hit-location)).
- **Criticals and fumbles** are read from the final roll too. Flipping 91 to 19 against a skill of 20 turns a failure into a success, not a critical; flipping 50 to 05 against a skill of 55 turns a success into a critical.
- **Opposed tests:** both sides can spend Luck. The defender decides after the attacker.

## NPCs

Ordinary NPCs don't have Luck. The GM can give a major villain or rival a small pool (1–3 per scene) to make them feel like a match for the heroes.
